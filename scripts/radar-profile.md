# Radar profile — who these opportunities are for

Pablo: **18**, lives in **Colombia** (Colombian passport), **undergraduate CS student**.
Founder who ships AI agents and software. Into robotics: humanoids, manipulation, robot arms.

Core rule: high upside, open to someone in Colombia, unusually strong people, real
funding, little wasted time. Ten great finds beat fifty okay ones.

## Hard filters (drop if any fails)

- Must allow an 18-year-old.
- Must allow applicants from Colombia, or be globally open.
- Must allow undergrads. Leaving or dropping out only for Thiel-level upside.
- Deadline still open, or the next cycle is predictable and worth tracking.
- Field: software, AI/ML, startups, founder programs, robotics/humanoids/manipulation,
  research, or hackathons/technical competitions.
- Real institution, company, university, accelerator, foundation or conference.
  No pay-to-play.

## What wins

- **Funding, weighed against time.** $1,000 travel for a 4-day event can beat $15k for
  four months of relocation. Fully funded = flights + housing + program costs covered.
- **People.** Will he meet people far better than the ones he normally can? YC, Stanford,
  MIT, Berkeley, ICRA, IROS, Humanoids, EPFL, Max Planck, Neo, Thiel, Start-Up Chile and
  major global hackathons are the bar.
- **Travel with a point.** Extra credit for funded trips to SF/Bay Area, NYC, Boston,
  Europe, Switzerland and big Asian tech hubs — only when the room is dense with talent.
- **Startup programs** must give money, customers, investors, elite founders,
  cofounders, distribution or real signal. "Attend some workshops" is worthless.
- **Robotics order:** humanoids / manipulation / arms > general robotics >
  embedded/hardware > generic IoT. Skip sensor projects, generic Arduino, industrial
  monitoring.
- **Stage fit:** he has MVPs, no revenue, no incorporated company. Flag anything that
  needs traction, revenue or a company.
- **Long shots are fine.** A 3% shot at Thiel is worth an application.

## Examples

- 3-day Stanford hackathon + funded flight: excellent.
- 1-week founder residency + funded trip: excellent.
- 10-week summer research + stipend: potentially excellent.
- 4 months full-time abroad for $15k: questionable.
- 2 years requiring dropout: only Thiel-level.
